The Ceremony

Igba Nkwu

The wine carrying · from 10am

  1. I

    Arrival & Welcome

    Guests are received and seated.

  2. II

    Breaking of Kola Iwa ọjị

    The elders bless the gathering.

  3. III

    Introduction of Families

    Both families are formally presented.

  4. IV

    Presentation of Wine & Gifts

    The customary items are brought forward.

  5. V

    The Bride's Entrance

    Chidimma is led in by her maidens.

  6. VI

    The Wine Carrying Igba Nkwu

    She searches for Abuchi, and kneels to offer him the palm wine.

  7. VII

    Blessing of the Couple

    Elders pray over the new union.

  8. VIII

    Feasting & Celebration

    Music, dancing and a shared meal.
